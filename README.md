# MedEase

## Introduction

MedEase is a web-based Pharmacy Management System designed to simplify and automate the daily operations of medical stores, wholesalers, distributors and hospital pharmacies. Traditional pharmacies often manage billing, inventory, purchases and accounts manually, which can result in calculation errors, stock mismanagement and difficulty in tracking expired medicines. MedEase provides a single platform where pharmacy owners can manage their complete business workflow.

## Problem It Solves

* Manual billing and invoice generation
* Difficulty tracking medicine stock
* Expired and near-expiry medicines
* Incorrect inventory calculations
* GST calculation and reporting issues
* Difficulty maintaining purchase and sales records
* Lack of centralized business reports
* Manual record keeping

## Significance & Impact

* Reduce manual work
* Generate invoices quickly
* Track medicine batches and expiry dates
* Maintain accurate inventory
* Manage GST-related information
* Generate sales and purchase reports
* Improve overall business efficiency

## Methodology

### Agile Software Development Methodology

The Agile methodology can be used because the system consists of multiple independent modules that can be developed and tested incrementally. The development process follows an iterative cycle of requirements, design, development, testing, feedback and improvement.

**Requirement Analysis → UI/Design → Development → Testing → Feedback → Improvement**
