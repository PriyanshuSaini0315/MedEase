Questions chemists actually ask

Do I need to change my current invoice numbering?

No. Tell us your last invoice number when you sign up and MediFlow continues counting from there, so your GST filing stays continuous.

Can I bring in my existing stock and prices?

Yes — send us your current stock sheet (Excel or even a photo of your register) and we load it in during setup, batch numbers and expiry included.

What happens if the internet drops mid-sale?

Billing keeps working offline on the same device and syncs the moment the connection is back. No sale is ever lost.

Is my shop's data visible to anyone else?

No. Every shop's stock, invoices and party ledgers are isolated from every other shop on MediFlow — nobody else can see your numbers.

Can one account cover more than one counter or branch?

Yes, on the Professional and Enterprise plans. Each counter gets its own biller login, and stock can be tracked per branch or shared across all of them.
